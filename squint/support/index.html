SQUINT

Can you guess who it is from just a tiny, blurry image?

About the Game

Squint is a visual trivia game that challenges your recognition skills. Each round starts with a tiny, heavily blurred image — and you have to guess who or what it is from multiple-choice clues. Guess wrong and the image reveals a little more. Think fast — you only get 3 attempts and 20 seconds per round.

How to Play

  • Pick a category or play All Categories
  • A tiny, blurred image appears — squint hard!
  • Choose from 4 clue-based answer options
  • Wrong answer? The image zooms out and new clues appear
  • Score up to 500 points per round (10 rounds per game)
  • Play solo or challenge up to 4 friends in Elimination mode

Categories

13 categories spanning Hip Hop Legends, Pop Stars, NBA Legends, Hollywood A-List, R&B Icons, Iconic Women of America, World Leaders, Tech Titans, Sports GOATs, TV & Streaming Stars, World Landmarks, Everyday Objects, and more.

Privacy Policy

Squint does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your scores are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.
